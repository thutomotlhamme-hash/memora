# Memora marketing kit

| File | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [01-positioning.md](01-positioning.md) | Who we serve, what we promise, tone of voice, ethics and objections |
| [02-launch-plan.md](02-launch-plan.md) | 90-day plan, weekly rhythm, scoreboard, money, what to build next |
| [03-copy-bank.md](03-copy-bank.md) | Ready-to-paste WhatsApp messages, church/printer/society pitches, ads, TikTok scripts, FAQ |
| [04-content-calendar.md](04-content-calendar.md) | 30 days of posts |
| [05-partners.md](05-partners.md) | Partner programme for churches, printers, burial societies |
| [assets/](assets) | Social images, WhatsApp Status images, an A5 flyer and an A4 poster (PDF) |

Assets are generated by `node marketing/build-assets.mjs` (needs Playwright + Chromium). The designs live in that script; edit and re-run. Set `SITE=https://your-domain` to point the QR codes and URLs at your own domain.
