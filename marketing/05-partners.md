# Partner programme

Partners are people families already trust at the moment a funeral is organised.

| Partner | Why they care | What they get | Ask |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Churches** (secretary, pastor, funeral committee) | Fewer calls about times and directions; a dignified programme | R100 per published memorial to the church (or a donation in the family's name), plus a QR poster for the office | Recommend Memora to families; build the programme with them |
| **Printers / copy shops** | Clean final PDFs, fewer late-night redos, more print jobs | R100 per published memorial they refer; a counter poster | Put the poster up; refer design requests |
| **Burial societies / stokvels** | A member benefit at no cost | R100 per member family that publishes | 5 minutes at a meeting; a WhatsApp group post |
| **Florists, caterers, tombstone makers** | A useful referral to give families | R100 per published memorial | Hand out the A5 flyer |
| **Community WhatsApp admins** | Helping their community | A free memorial for their own family if ever needed | Share the launch post once |

## How it works for now (no code needed)

1. Give each partner a **short name code**, e.g. `STMARKS`, `PRINTPRO`.
2. The family mentions the code on WhatsApp or in the contact form, or the partner messages you the family's name.
3. On the 1st of each month, pay partners by EFT for last month's published memorials and send a thank-you with their count.
4. Keep a simple sheet: partner, code, contact, bank details, referrals, amount paid.

**Next build:** a partner-code field at checkout and a partner view on `/admin`, so this becomes automatic.

## Rules

- Partners must **never** cold-message bereaved families or use death notices to find them.
- Partners recommend; the family chooses.
- Commission is paid only on published memorials (not refunded ones).
