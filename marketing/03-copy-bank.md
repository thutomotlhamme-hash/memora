## 9. FAQ answers (for comments and DMs)

- **How much?** R899 once-off per memorial. Building and previewing are free.
- **How long is it online?** A full year from publishing, so you can update it for the unveiling. You can download the keepsake book to keep forever.
- **Can I print it?** Yes. Download a print-ready A4 programme PDF with the QR code and take it to any printer.
- **Is it private?** Nothing is public until you publish, and memorials aren't listed on Google. Only people with the link or QR code can see it.
- **What if the time changes?** Change it once and the link, QR code and downloads all update.
- **Do guests need an app?** No. It opens in any phone's browser. The QR code works with the normal camera.
